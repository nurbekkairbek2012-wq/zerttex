# Interview questionnaire (MVP weeks 1–2)

Used in the first two weeks of the MVP plan to check the four problems from the case (unclear booking, trust, foreign card, unclear inclusions) with real people and to test interest in a chat-based booking assistant.
Respondents: Indian students and other foreign travelers in Kazakhstan + tour guides and travel agents.
Format: Google Form or short interview, anonymous, about 4 minutes.

Intro text: "We are a student team from Astana preparing a hackathon project about tourism in Kazakhstan. The survey is anonymous and takes about 4 minutes."

## Part A. Travelers (English)

1. You are: foreign tourist / foreign student or expat in Kazakhstan / tour guide / travel agent
2. Your country: ___
3. Age: under 18 / 18–24 / 25–34 / 35–44 / 45+
4. How do you usually find tours when you travel abroad? (multiple) Instagram or YouTube / Google / GetYourGuide, Viator or Klook / MakeMyTrip or another travel app / friends or family / travel agent
5. Before today, had you heard of Charyn Canyon, Kolsai Lakes, Turkestan or Burabay? yes, most of them / one or two / none
6. How easy would it be for you to book a 2–5 day guided trip in Kazakhstan today? 1 (very hard) – 5 (very easy)
7. What would stop you most from booking with a small local tour company abroad? (pick up to 2) unclear how to book / not sure it is trustworthy / my card or payment may not work / unclear what is included / language barrier / price
8. Has an online payment to a foreign company ever failed for you? yes, more than once / yes, once / no
9. How would you prefer to pay? Visa or Mastercard online / Apple Pay or Google Pay / UPI or a local wallet / cash on arrival / bank transfer
10. What would make you trust a company you have never heard of? (pick up to 3) verified reviews / official licence and insurance shown / video intro of the guide / free cancellation or refund guarantee / fast replies on WhatsApp / secure international payment / live trip sharing with family
11. Would you use an AI assistant (WhatsApp + website) that answers 24/7 and lets you book and pay in your currency in about 5 minutes? definitely / probably / not sure / probably not / no
12. What must be clearly shown as "included" before you pay? (multiple) transport / meals, including vegetarian options / entry fees / hotel / guide's language / pickup time and place / insurance
13. A fair price per person per day for an all-inclusive guided nature trip: under $50 / $50–80 / $80–120 / over $120
14. (open) What would make you choose Kazakhstan over another destination?

## Part B. Guides and travel agents (можно на русском)

G1. Как иностранцы сейчас вас находят и связываются с вами?
G2. Что иностранцы чаще всего спрашивают перед бронированием?
G3. Почему иностранные брони чаще всего срываются?
G4. Были ли проблемы с оплатой от иностранцев? Какие?
G5. Сколько часов в день уходит на переписку с клиентами? Помог бы AI-ассистент?
